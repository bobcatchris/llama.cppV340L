NUMERICS NOTE (the insight was RIGHT; the delivery was WRONG): relL2 = 0.00e+00 — madmix-wpair is
  BIT-IDENTICAL to V0/REF on device, exactly as the mantissa-exactness argument predicted (e2m1 x e4m3
  in fp16 is exact; mad-mix's fl32 product+acc equals V0's fmaf). The mad-mix op itself works and is
  accurate on this part. What failed is RESOURING, not math or the ISA feature.

WHY (mechanism): the GEMM tile's 32-accumulator working set is irreducible (64r x 128t / 256 threads),
  and the 2048-op asm MAC body is opaque to the scheduler — it cannot interleave the chains with the
  decode stream the way it does for V0's transparent fmaf nest. Result: 256 VGPR + spill + occupancy 1
  block/CU (V0: 128 VGPR, 2 blocks), and the ~2000-op LDS/addressing/wait floor (454 waits vs V0's 212;
  368 ds_reads; 230 v_lshl_or address ops) now dominates an issue stream that mad-mix only halved. V0's
  wasted 52% decode stream was NEVER free lunch — it was the scheduler's latency-hiding material. The
  same pattern that killed pk16 (its best resourcing point measured 1.34x under a 2.0x bar) kills this
  family at the resourcing stage before the MAC-density gain can even be quoted.
