## 1. What the tile is

Direct f16-weight GEMM: C[m][n] = sum_k X[m][k] * W[n][k] with weights resident f16
in HBM (load-time dequant outside the per-call cost; the deleted route tax is the
shipped per-call dequant of the whole weight slice, ~16 GB/die/ubatch of f16 writes,
E-012/E-013). Math: v_pk_fma_f16 packed along k-pairs, thread tile TY x TX half2
accumulators, LDS double buffers (A staged m-major b128, W staged kp-major with +4
bank pad), interleaved column ownership (thread owns n = cg + t*NC) so W-frag reads
hit distinct banks and A-frag reads broadcast within a wave phase. Split-K in z with
a fixed-order f32 reduce kernel (timed); ks auto: grid*ks filled toward 224 SIMDs,
slice stays KC-multiple. f32 out (M x N_d), no trailing convert kernel (the shipped
h16 route pays one).

All arms are template instantiations of one kernel; rb16 (rocBLAS hgemm, the shipped
route) is the control in every session.

## 2. Oracle (gate before any timing counted)

Two mirrors per (kc, flush, ks) class:
1. Host mirror of the exact per-output accumulation order (chunk-local flush
   schedule, per-slice f16 state, fixed-order f32 slice sum). The fused-half FMA is
   emulated in double precision - the f16 product (22-bit) plus the add is exact in
   f64, so one rounding reproduces v_pk_fma_f16 bit-exactly.
2. fp64 reference over the same f16 inputs; L2 rel err must be <= 2x the same-data
   rocBLAS h16 error.

Two real bugs caught by the oracle before any timing counted:
- 64-bit index math: (size_t) offsets compiled to v_mad_u64_u32 chains, ~500 extra
  VALU instructions per loop vs 512 fmas (2x instruction stream). Fix: 32-bit
  indexing throughout (max offset 5888*17408 < 2^31). Single biggest arm win.
- MT64 arm (a7): A-stage missed the M-tile row offset (read X rows 0..63 for both
  tiles): 50.8% of outputs wrong, L2 err ~1.0. Fix: add mb.
First oracle run also flagged the f32-precision fused emulation (1-2 f16 ulp drift,
3% of outputs) - replaced by the exact f64 emulation; after fixes: 90/90 arm-shape
gates PASS at mismatch 0.000%, max rel 0.0.

Numerics side result: tile L2-vs-f64 err 2.3-5.4e-3 vs shipped h16 ~4.9e-2 on the
same data = 10-19x more accurate (f16-acc over K/ks slices + f32 slice sums beats
full-K f16 accumulation).

## 6. Named negatives + mechanisms

- Register-prefetch pipeline arm (ldg->registers before compute, stlds after,
  1 barrier/chunk): REJECTED. VGPR exploded (a8 93 -> 246, a6 155 -> 256): 1-2
  waves/SIMD, every arm regressed (a6 4.25 -> 1.34 TF/s, a8 5.68 -> 3.67). The
  staging registers + scheduler copies cost more occupancy than the hidden HBM
  latency returns. Bulk stage + 2 barriers stands.
- NT128 arms (a5/a6): VGPR 132-155 and LDS 16.6-33.3 KB -> 1-2 WGs/CU; grid 45
  per K-slice is tail-heavy even with ks=8 (360 WGs of quarter-K work each).
- THT128 arms (a7/a9): 2 waves per barrier group, 128 threads - too little
  cross-wave work to cover staging; a7 also carries the MT64 gridDim.y=2 split.
- ffn_gate/up (K=5120, N_d~5.8k): parity, not a win (0.99-1.00x). rb16 is strong
  here (5.6-5.7) and the tile's residual costs (staging exposure at 2 waves/SIMD,
  LDS 1.5x oversubscription, split-K reduce pass) eat the full margin.

## 7. Ceiling named + next levers

a8 on ffn_gate = 5.60 TF/s/die = 26% of packed-fp16 peak (21.5). Model: per
wave-kpair a8 needs 48 LDS-cycles (4 b32 A-frag broadcasts + 8 b32 W-frag) per
128 VALU-cycles; the LDS is per-CU shared by 4 SIMDs -> 1.5x oversubscribed at
full occupancy -> ~14 TF/s model cap. Gap to measured: per-chunk staging exposure
(2 barriers/chunk, all waves lockstep at them, HBM latency exposed every chunk)
plus split-K reduce pass. Next levers in order: (1) deeper LDS buffering (3-4
buffers) or W-frag direct-from-L2 to cut exposure without the VGPR blowup the
register-prefetch arm showed; (2) shrink W-frag LDS cost (a8 already halves it
vs a3's A-heavy split - the a3 vs a8 delta confirms frag-side cost matters);
(3) fold the reduce into the epilogue via f32 atomics for small-N shapes.

## 8. Promotion recommendation

PROMOTE a8 as the W5 route: runtime-env-gated dispatch at the ggml-cuda.cu:2629
cublas fallback (E-012 option (a), static-once getenv pattern at 1600-1626),
per-shape geometry from the table above (a8 everywhere; a1 for N_d<=384 edges is
within noise of a8 - single geometry acceptable). Weight layout requirement: f16
row-major (n x K, k contiguous) resident per die - i.e. keep the load-time dequant
output as the weight's steady-state form (the E-012/E-013 route tax, ~16 GB/die/
ubatch of per-call dequant writes, is deleted on top of the +21% GEMM gain).
Numerics: strictly better than shipped (10-19x lower L2 vs f64), f32 out with no
trailing convert pass.
