## 3. MECHANISM: WHY THE SCHEDULE CEILINGS AT 70-120

Ranked by evidence weight; each entry names what rules alternatives out.

M1 (primary): memory-latency / MLP starvation of a short-runway schedule.
The kbx loop (mmvq.cu:575, `for (kbx = tid/(qi/vdr); kbx < blocks_per_row_x;
kbx += blocks_per_iter)`) gives each lane only 1-2 independent iterations at
K=5120 (blocks_per_row_x = 20; blocks_per_iter = vdr*nwarps*warp_size/qi =
16 for the iq3 class at nwarps=2, wave64). With ~600-cycle HBM/L2 latency
and the occupancy below, waves drain their loads and stall before the next
iteration issues. Evidence: E-106 cfull (compute deleted, ceiling survives,
70-120); E-106 "schedule-bound (1.25 kbx iterations of runway per lane)";
issue census 1446 inst/iter vs ~144 us demand vs ~800 us actual = 5.5x
slack (NOT issue-bound). At K=17408 (ffn_down class) runway is 4.25
iterations - the census's better BW classes should sit there, not at
K=5120 (testable in bench plan B2).

M2 (co-primary): register-limited occupancy. W7 occupancy dump on the real
templates: q4_K/q5_K/iq4_xs 101-104 regs = 4 CTAs/CU = 8 of 40 waves
(20%); iq3_s 83 = 6 CTAs (30%); iq3_xxs 64 = 8 CTAs (40%). Cause:
__launch_bounds__(nwarps*warp_size, 1) at mmvq.cu:477 requests ONE block
per SM (no VGPR cap), and the T=4 register load is heavy (share arms hold
dec[2][8] + per-token apply state; base arms hold per-token decode state).
Few waves x short runway = too few outstanding loads per CU. M1+M2 together
explain the cfull band; neither has been directly attacked (LDS-y was an
MLP attempt but added a round trip and lost; wide-x attacked width, not
concurrency, and x is only 12-20% of the stream - W6 named negative).

M3 (volume term, partially cashed): y-broadcast redundancy. Every CTA
re-reads the same per-token y window (mmvq.cu:571, y advanced per channel;
apply reads &y[j*stride_col_y + kby] at mmvq.cu:609/617 etc). Per wave-
iteration a warp touches 8 q8_1 blocks x 36 B = 288 B/token; per CTA per
launch: T x 2 warps x 20 kbx x 288 B ~= 46 KB at T=4, re-fetched by all
8704 CTAs (~279 MB of L2->L1 traffic vs 34 MB of unique weights on the W7
shape). The compiler does not CSE the two rows' copies (E-106, the reason
s2r exists); share+s2r already removed the row-pair duplication, and the
decode-once gates removed the per-token weight decode. What remains
uncashed: the T=4 y stream is still read once per CTA per token with no
cross-token hoist (see C3).

Ruled out (with citation, do not re-open):
- Uncoalesced weight reads: W6 A1 misalignment law (110/98 B strides put
  block bases in all residue classes; get_int_b2 ushort loads are the only
  legal widths - vecdotq.cuh:1453-1458 is dword+byte scattered but the
  sectors are shared within the 110 B block; amplification ~1.2x) and W6
  A2/W5 wide-x measured NOT-MOVEMENT (-0.9..-1.2%).
- VALU/decode issue cost: 5.5x issue slack (E-106); perm atom killed twice
  (W6: v_perm semantics invalid on gfx900 + capped by the stall bound).
- dp4a ISA wall: 6-VALU emulation is per-op optimal (gfx900 has no
  v_dot4; W6/q4_K note) - priced into the tax, not the ceiling (cfull has
  no dp4a and still ceilings at 70-120).
- Launch overhead: kernels are 0.5-5 ms at T=4; launch floor 12 us (W8
  floors). Not a term at this M.
- L2 thrash across dies: TP4 shards weights per die; the MMVQ kernels
  themselves have no cross-die traffic (RCCL boundaries are a separate
  21-24 ms pool, E-115).

## 4. CANDIDATES (ranked by expected-ms x risk; ms vs the 56.0 pool)

C1. RE-OPEN GGML_CUDA_MMVQ_IQ4XS_SHARE=1 (env only, zero code).
    Mechanism: the served iq4_xs arm is BASE (E-104 turned its share off on
    replica numbers of +5.3%); the real kernel says share is -18.7% at T=4
    (W7 final tree, oracle bit-exact, base-dup controls clean; also -5.4%
    at T=2). The ledger already calls it "the wrongly-disabled real win"
    (plan line ~3516).
    Expected: 10.9 ms x 0.187 = -2.0 ms/round. iq4_xs is also the best
    base class (75 GB/s served), and its qs is the one 8 B-aligned layout
    (W6), so the share arm composes with the least friction.
    Risk: zero-ish (env gate, twice-oracled on the served template).
    Bench recipe: B1 below, then served interleaved A/B window.

C2. WIRE THE W7-VERIFIED S2R SUBSET (env only; the gates exist, the E-117
    wiring fix is landed, but NO *_S2R var is set in the serving env - all
    campaign BASEENV script lines carry only the 6 *_SHARE gates).
    Mechanism: row-pair y-preload removes the un-CSE'd duplicate y loads
    (mmvq.cu:595-601, 625-631).
    Expected (W7 real-kernel T=4 increments vs the share arm, use THESE,
    not the W5-era comment numbers in mmvq.cu:1141-1143): iq3_xxs -6.3% x
    14.8 = -0.93 ms; iq3_s -2.9% x 17.6 = -0.51 ms; q4_K -0.3% and q5_K
    +0.2% = skip; iq4_xs s2r is +5.2% vs its base = skip (C1 takes share,
    not s2r). Total -1.4 ms/round.
    Risk: zero-ish (bit-exact per W7 A4; add GGML_CUDA_MMVQ_IQ3XXS_S2R=1 +
    GGML_CUDA_MMVQ_IQ3S_S2R=1 only).

C3. WIDE-S2R: HOIST ALL 4 TOKEN Y-PRELOADS BEFORE APPLY (new code, one
    template variant, env-gated).
    Mechanism: in the s2r arm the per-token preloads (mmvq.cu:596/626/656,
    `const mmvq_yw8 w = vec_dot_*_preload(&y[j*stride_col_y + kby], kqs)`)
    are unrolled but the compiler schedules them against use to cap VGPRs;
    a variant that issues all ncols_dst preloads first (4 x 36 B in flight
    per lane-iteration) multiplies y-side MLP by 4 inside the same
    schedule. This attacks M1+M3 directly and is bit-exact by construction
    (identical integer sequences, identical apply order; only load timing
    moves). Bound: cfull says the same schedule with all loads wide
    ceilings at 70-120, so the honest expectation is closing 54.7 toward
    ~70, not toward 327.
    Expected: +5-15% on the gated iq3 classes and iq4_xs = -2.5 to -6
    ms/round if M1 is as named; -0 if the compiler already hoists
    (the bench's OCC dump + a rocprof stall check will say which in one
    session).
    Risk: M (new arm in mmvq.cu + bench arm; oracle mandatory; watch VGPR
    growth: 4 preloads ~= +27 regs on iq3_s s2r's 68 - if CTAs/CU drops
    6 -> 4 the MLP win can eat itself; measure, do not assume).
    Bench recipe: B2.

C4. LIFT THE T=1-ONLY GLU FUSION BAN FOR T=4 (new code, moderate).
    Mechanism: served T=4 runs ffn_gate and ffn_up as TWO separate MMVQ
    launches that read the same y independently - fusion is refused at
    ncols_dst != 1 twice (host check ggml-cuda.cu:2648 and kernel-side
    GGML_ASSERT mmvq.cu:1205), although the kernel body already carries
    the full fusion epilogue (bias/gate/GLU, mmvq.cu:539-565, 840-867).
    Fusing gate+up at T=4 halves y traffic on the gate/up class (~41% of
    weight bytes: gate 0.59 + up 0.65 GB/die of 3.06) and removes one
    launch per pair. Simplest landable shape: base+fusion (no share/s2r
    composition) for c_ncols_dst 2..4 - mirrors the served T=1 fused
    template.
    Expected: if the E-106 x/y stream-mix tax (+41-100%) is halved on
    38% of bytes: -2 to -4.5 ms/round.
    Risk: M/L (graph-level change: the fusion handshake, dst ownership,
    split-buffer guard; bit-exactness provable - same per-token dots, the
    silu epilogue is the already-served T=1 code - but this touches op
    routing, so it needs the full A/B + oracle treatment and a day of
    care). Gate/up are ~45% of the IQ3_S ms pool, so the prize is real.

Killed-adjacent, not re-proposed: LDS-y staging (W7 named negative),
K-split (W6 geometry), perm atoms (W6 double kill), wide-x (W5/W6), ALN
relayout (W4), rpb=4 without s2r (derivation: 4 un-CSE'd y loads per
(kby,token) - strictly worse than s2r; rpb=4 WITH s2r is MLP-neutral and
loses CTAs/CU to dec[4][8] registers - dead on arrival, do not bench).

Static stack projection: C1+C2 = -3.4 ms env-only (56.0 -> ~52.6);
C3 (if it moves) -> ~47-50; C4 -> ~44-48. At the round level (129.0 ms
battery) that is +2.6% (env) to +6-8% (full stack) decode t/s. The
70-120 GB/s ceiling family itself stays (that is schedule physics on
gfx900 wave64 for these layouts); reaching past it means format-level
change (owner-guarded) or a tile/MMQ schedule that served already rejects
at T<=4.
