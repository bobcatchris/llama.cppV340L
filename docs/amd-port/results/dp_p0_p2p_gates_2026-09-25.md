## THE ONE-LINE SUMMARY

The 12/12 peer-access refusal (W31/W38) is caused by FIRMWARE-ENABLED ACS P2P
redirect bits on every bridge of the GPU fabric PLUS a POLICY WHITELIST entry
missing for this consumer host bridge. Both are changeable: the ACS bits are
runtime-clearable with setpci (no rebuild, same-card pairs unblock); the
whitelist is a one-line kernel patch (cross-card pairs). No silicon limitation
has been demonstrated anywhere in the chain.
