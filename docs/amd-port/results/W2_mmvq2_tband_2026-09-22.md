## HEADLINE: decode-once/dot-per-j ("share") is a 34-46% kernel win at T=2-4

The shipped mul_mat_vec_q calls vec_dot_q_cuda once PER TOKEN: the 8
constant-LUT lookups + sign chain (y-independent work, the -28%/-41% terms of
the E-006 ablation) are re-executed ncols_dst times per weight block. share
computes the 8 signed quads ONCE per (kbx, lane) and the per-token loop keeps
only the 8 x-int loads + 8 dp4a, in the same order. BIT-EXACT: per-token
integer sequence unchanged, same (d*ds)*sumi float mul order -> zero
acceptance price (the ride is free when bit-exact).

## Limiter readings

1. T-scaling of the SHIPPED kernel is steep (T=4 costs 3.64x T=1) while the
   byte floor is T-invariant (~209 us, weight-dominated): per-token decode
   re-execution dominates the verify band. This is the T-scaling ablation
   naming the decode chain as the binding term, from the cost side.
2. share flattens the curve to 1.19x/1.53x/2.08x (T=2/3/4 vs T=1).
3. aln (48 B aligned y, uint4 operand loads) REGRESSES vs share at T=2-3 but
   WINS at T=4 (757.7 vs 868.9, -12.8%): the x-operand byte stream (T*32 B
   per 110 B weights) only overtakes the decode term at T=4. aln requires a
   q8_1 producer-side relayout (arm-d, quantize is ~7.1% of decode step) -
   banked as the named next lever, NOT shipped (cross-cutting layout change).
4. Residual after share at T=4: 757.7 us = 3.6x the 209 us floor. The
   residual is the per-lane dependent decode chain itself (LUT + emulated
   dp4a), which the 09-21 receipts ISA-walled at T=1 (no dot instructions on
   gfx900 - today's compile probe: v_dot2_i32_i16, v_dot2_f32_f16,
   v_dot4_i32_iu8, v_dot4_i32_i8 ALL refuse to assemble with rocm-6.2.0).
   Arm (b) of the relaunch is DEAD by ISA probe, consistent with 09-21.

## Oracle-gate defects found and closed (can-fail-before-can-green)

1. Readback undersize: the first build copied ORACLE_ROWS*TMAX floats while
   indexing token planes at j*N -> token >= 1 read out of bounds (looked like
   a kernel bug, was an instrument bug). Gate refused everything (correct
   failure direction).
2. NaN-blind green: `rel >= gate` is FALSE for NaN, so an all-NaN arm
   green-lights itself. The aln arm hit exactly this (ds read through the
   36 B block_q8_1 view of a 48 B-strided buffer). Fixed: fail condition is
   now `!(rel < 1e-4)` (NaN fails) and max-tracking is NaN-aware. Lesson for
   every oracle in the campaign: NaN inputs must FAIL the gate, never pass.

## Served projection (STATIC-COUNT CLASS - candidate, not a price; N-19 law)

Campaign census: MMVQ = ~77% of decode kernel time at 200k. E-092: MTP k=3,
accept 0.66667 -> verify width T=4 every round (draft always proposes 3).
If served MMVQ at T=4 takes the share win (-43.0%) and the bigger
share+aln (-50.3%, needs the producer change), decode-step kernel time moves
by 0.77 x -0.430 = -33.1% (share) / -38.7% (share+aln), i.e. served decode
t/s upper bound x1.49 / x1.63 - BEFORE the T-mix, the other-tensor mix, and
the smaller-tensor tax (T3 class, ~12-15%) are priced. A served A/B is the
adjudicator; this projection names the candidate only.
