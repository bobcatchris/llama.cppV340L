## Findings

1. TRUE per-device MTP cost at TP3/200k = 1061-1255 MiB/die at boot-ready
   (mean 1125.4), 968-976 MiB/die post-battery (mean 972.4). The >2 GB/device
   hypothesis does NOT reproduce at 200k scale. Cost is nearly ctx-independent
   vs the TP2/10k record (1125.4 vs 1072.8 MiB/die, +4.9% for 20x ctx),
   consistent with the E-030 draft-context-buffer attribution (weights+KV only
   ~90 MiB).
2. Die 0 carries ~195 MiB more MTP cost than dies 1/2 (1255.3 vs 1061.3/1059.7)
   - rank-0 draft-side extra (draft compute/output buffers), not KV (KV is
   symmetric in-split).
3. Arm B boot-ready 7809.9/7582.1/7634.2 matches the E-031 handed-over TP3
   control record (7809.8/7582.0/7634.2) within 0.1 MiB - cross-boot,
   cross-desk, cross-day reproducibility.
4. Operational ceiling: the MTP-ON arm serves within 10.3-25.1 MiB/die of the
   die ceiling at serving peak. Quantifies the E-041/E-044 tile-OOM mechanism:
   any per-call pool alloc > ~10 MiB aborts die 0 at the 200k served point.
5. Served decode (8k-10k cell, 128 tok, cache_prompt=false): OFF 12.23 t/s vs
   ON 15.12 t/s = +23.6% (>= 5% unambiguous class; matches the baseline
   "untraced_no_spec_reference_tps" 12.24 for OFF). Accept 0.0 vs 0.66667;
   greedy determinism sha 4beb1ba25219ee9b byte-identical in BOTH arms
   (matches the E-027 gate of record); needle 3/3 both arms. Single-rep prefill
   delta -6.2% (123.14 OFF vs 115.50 ON) noted; not this lane's cell.
   Arm A FAIL verdict (decode/canary) is the expected control signature - the
   OFF arm is a measurement control, not a promotion candidate.
