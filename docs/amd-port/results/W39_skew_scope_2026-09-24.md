## 3. W-COUPLING LAW (interaction with the W37 kernel levers)

The rate spread is multiplicative, so the ABSOLUTE skew is proportional
to per-boundary work W: skew = W_max - W_balanced = 45.2 us at W_max =
579.4 us, i.e. skew = 0.078 x W_max.  Consequences, quantified:

- Every W37 compute saving carries a skew-compression rider: Opp 1
  (MMVQ schedule lift, -12.2 to -17.7 ms) shrinks per-boundary W by
  ~90-130 us, so skew drops 45.2 -> ~38-35 us: an EXTRA ~0.95-1.4
  ms/cycle beyond the direct saving.  Opp 2 (-2.5 to -3.5 ms) carries
  ~+0.2-0.3 ms.  No separate window needed - it rides inside the Opp
  windows' primary metric.
- Conversely, the clock-compression prize SHRINKS as compute improves:
  if Opp 1 lands -12 ms, full equalization is worth 6.1 -> ~5.2 ms, not
  6.1.  The campaign should not double-count the same microsecond in
  the W37 and skew columns - this receipt books the rider here so the
  columns stay additive.
- Draft/catch-up boundaries (8 of 136) carry ~5x smaller W, so their
  skew is proportionally smaller; the verify pass owns the term.

## 6. VERDICT

inside the NCCL kernels, not wall time (W10); the wall-visible skew is
the 45.2 us/boundary per-die RATE spread = 3.0-6.1 ms/cycle of honest

in exact quarters (divisibility ledger, section 2.1) and the split

clock/power state; its direct actuator is dead (E-149).  What remains

fire-ready), the W-coupling rider on the W37 kernel levers, and the
queued C2/soak system-state items.  No code arm drafted - nothing left
in this family needs one, and the branch is better for it.
